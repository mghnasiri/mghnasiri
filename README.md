# Mohammad Ghafourian Nasiri

**PhD Researcher in Operations Research | Healthcare Systems Optimization**

I am a PhD student at [Universite Laval](https://www.ulaval.ca/) and a member of [CIRRELT](https://www.cirrelt.ca/) (Interuniversity Research Centre on Enterprise Networks, Logistics and Transportation), working under the supervision of Prof. Paolo Landa. My research focuses on applying mathematical optimization and operations research methods to improve healthcare delivery and hospital operations.

---

## Research Interests

- **Healthcare Operations Research** -- staff scheduling, resource allocation, and service-level optimization in hospital settings
- **Mathematical Programming** -- integer programming, mixed-integer linear programming (MILP), and stochastic optimization
- **Metaheuristics** -- adaptive large neighborhood search (ALNS), variable neighborhood search, and hybrid approaches for combinatorial optimization
- **Vehicle Routing & Logistics** -- capacitated VRP variants, routing heuristics, and logistics network design
- **Data-Driven Decision Making** -- integrating analytics with optimization models for real-world operational improvement

## Technical Skills
